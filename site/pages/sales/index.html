Questions before you start

Good questions deserve clear answers.

How long does each tub last?

Each tub contains 30 soft chews. A small dog takes 1 daily, a medium dog takes 2, and a large dog takes 3. That is why we suggest 1, 2 or 3 tubs for about a month.

How soon will I notice a difference?

Every dog is different, so there is no guaranteed timeline. Give Daily Gut consistently as directed and watch the overall pattern. See your veterinarian for symptoms that are sudden, severe or persistent.

Does this treat allergies, yeast or parasites?

No. Daily Gut supports normal digestive and immune function. Allergies, infections and parasites need appropriate veterinary diagnosis and treatment.

What if my dog is picky?

The soft chew can be offered by hand or crumbled over food. It contains pork, fish and dairy ingredients, so review the label and ask your veterinarian if your dog has food sensitivities.

How does the subscription work?

Your selected supply ships and bills monthly. You can skip, change or cancel anytime online through your subscription account. There is no minimum commitment.

What if it is not right for my dog?

Your first order is protected by our 30-day money-back guarantee, even if the tub is open. See the refund policy for details.
