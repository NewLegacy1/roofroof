Honey

What does Daily Gut do?

Daily Gut is a digestive support supplement. It's made to help support healthy digestion, gut balance and normal stool quality as part of your dog's daily routine.*

How do I give it?

Offer the chews by hand like a treat, or crumble them over food. Use the serving guide and the label on your tub for the daily amount.

How soon will I notice a difference?

Every dog is different. Give Daily Gut every day as directed; consistency matters more than anything. If digestive problems are sudden, severe or don't go away, see your vet.

Is it a medicine?

No. Daily Gut is a supplement for everyday care. It isn't intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. If your dog has a health condition, is on medication, or is pregnant or nursing, check with your vet first.
